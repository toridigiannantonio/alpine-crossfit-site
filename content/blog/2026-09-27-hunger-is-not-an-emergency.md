## Hunger Is Not an Emergency

> It is the power of the mind to be unconquerable.
>
> — Seneca, Letters to Lucilius 78

The body sends urgent signals about mild things. Tired, hungry, uncomfortable, bored — all of them arrive with the same insistence as real danger.

Learning to sit with a signal without obeying it immediately is most of what training teaches. The barbell is a rehearsal.

**Today's challenge:** Finish today's workout without stopping for anything that isn't genuinely necessary.
