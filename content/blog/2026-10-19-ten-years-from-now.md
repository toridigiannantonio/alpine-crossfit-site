## Ten Years From Now

> Let us prepare our minds as if we'd come to the very end of life.
>
> — Seneca, Letters to Lucilius 101

Ask what you want to be able to do at seventy and the whole picture reorganises. Not a max lift — the ability to travel, to lift a grandchild, to get up off the floor unaided.

Almost nothing you'd do to chase a number this month serves that. Almost everything you'd do to stay uninjured does.

**Today's challenge:** Choose one thing today with your seventy-year-old self in mind.
