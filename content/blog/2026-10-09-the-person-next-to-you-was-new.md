## The Person Next to You Was New

> We are all limbs of one great body.
>
> — Seneca, Letters to Lucilius 95

The person doing muscle-ups was once the person who couldn't hang from the bar. That transition is not a story they tell, but it happened.

Nobody in a good gym is looking down at a beginner, because everyone remembers being one clearly.

**Today's challenge:** Ask someone stronger than you what their first month looked like.
