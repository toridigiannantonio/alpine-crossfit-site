## Scaling Is the Skill

> Know thyself.
>
> — inscribed at Delphi, cited by the Stoics

Anyone can pick a weight that's too heavy — it takes no judgment at all, only appetite. Picking the weight that lets you move well for the whole workout takes an honest read of yourself.

That read is a trainable skill, and it is worth more over a decade than any single lift you'll ever make.

**Today's challenge:** Scale one movement down today on purpose and finish the workout unbroken.
