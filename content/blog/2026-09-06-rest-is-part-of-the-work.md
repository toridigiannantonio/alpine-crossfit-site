## Rest Is Part of the Work

> The mind must be given relaxation; it will rise improved and sharper after resting.
>
> — Seneca, On Tranquility of Mind 17

Seneca was writing about the mind, but the body follows the same rule. Adaptation happens between sessions, not during them. Train seven days a week and you accumulate fatigue, not fitness.

The sauna and the cold plunge aren't a reward for the hard part. They are the hard part's other half.

**Today's challenge:** Take a genuine rest day this week without calling it lazy.
