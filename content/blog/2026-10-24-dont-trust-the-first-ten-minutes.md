## Don't Trust the First Ten Minutes

> Nothing happens to anybody which he is not fitted by nature to bear.
>
> — Marcus Aurelius, Meditations 5.18

Plenty of good sessions start badly. The legs feel dead in the warm-up and fine by the third set; the shoulder that ached at the door is forgotten by the second round.

Judging a workout from the opening minutes is how people talk themselves out of the ones that would have gone well.

**Today's challenge:** If today starts badly, give it fifteen minutes before deciding anything.
