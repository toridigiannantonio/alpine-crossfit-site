## The Whiteboard Is Not a Verdict

> Never say about anything, 'I have lost it,' but say, 'I have given it back.'
>
> — Epictetus, Enchiridion 11

You slept badly, you ate at nine, it's the third session this week and your legs know it. The score reflects all of that, and none of it is character.

Bad days in a long training history are not failures. They are the price of having a long training history.

**Today's challenge:** Look at a score you weren't happy with and name one condition that explains it.
