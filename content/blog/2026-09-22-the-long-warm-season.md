## The Long Warm Season

> Nothing great is created suddenly, any more than a bunch of grapes or a fig.
>
> — Epictetus, Discourses 1.15

Epictetus points out that a fig needs time — blossom, fruit, ripening — and none of it can be hurried by wanting it more. Bodies work the same way.

The athlete who added a little every month for three years is unrecognisable. No single month of that would have impressed anyone.

**Today's challenge:** Look back at what you could do a year ago rather than what you can't do today.
