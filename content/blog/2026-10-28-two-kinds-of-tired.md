## Two Kinds of Tired

> Nothing is burdensome if taken lightly, and nothing need arouse one's irritation so long as one doesn't make it bigger.
>
> — Seneca, Letters to Lucilius

Muscular fatigue is dull, symmetrical and fades. Joint pain is sharp, one-sided and follows you home. Treating them the same is how small problems become long ones.

The skill is not toughness. It's accurate reporting, to yourself and to your coach.

**Today's challenge:** If something is sharp or one-sided today, say so out loud.
