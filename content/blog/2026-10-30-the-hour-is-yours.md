## The Hour Is Yours

> Hold every hour in your grasp. Lay hold of today's task, and you will not need to depend so much upon tomorrow's.
>
> — Seneca, Letters to Lucilius 1

Most of the day belongs to other people. Work takes its share, family takes its share, and what's left gets spent on whatever asks loudest. The training hour is one of the few that can be genuinely defended.

Defending it is not selfish. It is the hour that makes you useful in all the others — steadier, stronger, better slept, less brittle when something goes wrong.

**Today's challenge:** Put next week's sessions in your calendar as appointments, not intentions.
