## What Consistency Actually Looks Like

> The soul becomes dyed with the colour of its thoughts.
>
> — Marcus Aurelius, Meditations 5.16

There's no dramatic week. There's a Tuesday, and then another Tuesday, and gradually the weights on the bar are numbers you'd have found absurd a year ago.

Nobody photographs the middle of that process, which is why it feels like it isn't happening.

**Today's challenge:** Count the sessions you've done in the last month. Just count them.
