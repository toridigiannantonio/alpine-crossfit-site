## Old Injuries, New Rules

> Accept the things to which fate binds you.
>
> — Marcus Aurelius, Meditations 6.39

A knee that went twenty years ago is a fact, not an argument. Fighting it produces flare-ups; ignoring it produces flare-ups.

Working with it — different depth, different tempo, different volume — produces training you can keep doing. That's the whole aim.

**Today's challenge:** Tell your coach about one old injury they don't know about yet.
