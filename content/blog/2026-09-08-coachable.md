## Coachable

> If anyone tells you that a certain person speaks ill of you, do not make excuses, but answer: he was ignorant of my other faults, else he would not have mentioned these alone.
>
> — Epictetus, Enchiridion 33

A coach walks over mid-set and changes your setup. Two reactions are available: explain why you were doing it that way, or just try it.

The second one costs your ego something and buys you years of progress. The first protects a version of you that isn't getting any stronger.

**Today's challenge:** Take one cue today without explaining yourself first.
