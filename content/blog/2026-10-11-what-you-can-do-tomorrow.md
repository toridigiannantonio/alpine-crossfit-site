## What You Can Do Tomorrow

> He who is everywhere is nowhere; he who lives everywhere lives nowhere.
>
> — Seneca, Letters to Lucilius 2

The right measure of a session is not how destroyed you feel afterwards. It's whether you can come back and do good work the next day.

Wrecking yourself once a week is easy. Building something takes the other four days being possible.

**Today's challenge:** Finish today feeling like you could have done a little more.
