## What the Clock Doesn't Measure

> It is not the man who has too little, but the man who craves more, that is poor.
>
> — Seneca, Letters to Lucilius 2

Two people finish the same workout in nine minutes. One held a hollow position on every rep; the other threw their back at it. The whiteboard shows one number and hides everything that mattered.

The clock is a useful liar. Use it for pacing, then ignore it when it starts telling you who you are.

**Today's challenge:** Pick one round today where you deliberately go slower to move better.
