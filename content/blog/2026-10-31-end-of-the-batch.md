## End of the Batch

> Do every act of your life as though it were the last act of your life.
>
> — Marcus Aurelius, Meditations 2.5

Sixty mornings of borrowed Stoicism won't move a barbell. The reading is the easy half, and it's finished now.

What's left is the part that was always going to be the work: turning up tomorrow, and the day after, and doing something unremarkable properly.

**Today's challenge:** Book your next session before you close this page.
