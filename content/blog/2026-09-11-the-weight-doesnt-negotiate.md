## The Weight Doesn't Negotiate

> Things are not what we wish them to be, but what they are.
>
> — Epictetus, Discourses

You can talk yourself into almost anything. You cannot talk a loaded barbell into being lighter. It sits there indifferent to your reasoning, and either you move it or you don't.

That indifference is why lifting is clarifying. An hour a day with something that cannot be argued with is good practice for the rest of life.

**Today's challenge:** Choose one lift today and let the outcome be simply true, without a story attached.
