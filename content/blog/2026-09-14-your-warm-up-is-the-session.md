## Your Warm-Up Is the Session

> The beginning is the most important part of the work.
>
> — attributed to Plato, Republic

The warm-up is where everyone's attention is thinnest and where the day is quietly decided. Rushed, it becomes the reason a lift feels wrong forty minutes later.

Treat it as the session's opening argument rather than a toll to be paid, and the heavy part gets easier for free.

**Today's challenge:** Do the whole warm-up today at the pace it was actually written for.
