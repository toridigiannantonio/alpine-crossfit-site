## The Value of Being Bad at Something

> It is impossible for a man to learn what he thinks he already knows.
>
> — Epictetus, Discourses 2.17

Being genuinely bad at a movement means every session produces visible improvement. That's a rate of return you can't buy back later.

Experienced athletes pay for tiny gains. Beginners get large ones for free and often resent them.

**Today's challenge:** Pick the movement you're worst at and do it first today.
