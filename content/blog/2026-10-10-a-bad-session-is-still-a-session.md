## A Bad Session Is Still a Session

> No great thing is created suddenly.
>
> — Epictetus, Discourses 1.15

The instinct after a poor session is to write it off. But the record that matters is not your best day — it's how bad your worst day is allowed to be.

Turning up and doing something mediocre keeps the streak, keeps the habit and keeps the adaptation. Nothing about that is wasted.

**Today's challenge:** Give yourself credit for a session you'd otherwise have dismissed.
