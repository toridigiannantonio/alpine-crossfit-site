## The Cost of Optimizing

> While we wait for life, life passes.
>
> — Seneca, Letters to Lucilius 1

Reading about training feels like training. It has the same seriousness and none of the sweat, which is precisely what makes it dangerous.

A mediocre plan executed on Tuesday beats an excellent plan you're still refining on Friday.

**Today's challenge:** Stop researching one thing today and just do the workout as written.
