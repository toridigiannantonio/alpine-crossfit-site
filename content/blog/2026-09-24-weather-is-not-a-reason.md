## Weather Is Not a Reason

> No man is free who is not master of himself.
>
> — Epictetus, Discourses

Snow on the road, a long day, a slight ache, an early meeting. Every one of them is true, and every one of them is also the reason nothing changes.

You don't have to defeat the excuse with willpower. You just have to notice that you're the one generating it.

**Today's challenge:** Name the excuse you use most often. Say it out loud. Then go.
