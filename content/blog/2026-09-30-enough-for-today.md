## Enough for Today

> Confine yourself to the present.
>
> — Marcus Aurelius, Meditations 7.29

The urge to make up for a missed week in a single session is how people get hurt. The body doesn't do arrears.

Today's session is only today's. Do it properly and let it be the whole ambition.

**Today's challenge:** Do exactly what's programmed today. No extra, no catching up.
