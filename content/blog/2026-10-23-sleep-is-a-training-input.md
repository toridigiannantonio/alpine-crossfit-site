## Sleep Is a Training Input

> Sleep is a part of nature, like eating and drinking.
>
> — Marcus Aurelius, Meditations

Nothing in a program survives four hours of sleep. Not the weight, not the pace, not the mood you bring into the room.

It's the least glamorous variable and the one with the largest effect. Fix it and every other input works better.

**Today's challenge:** Move your bedtime thirty minutes earlier tonight.
