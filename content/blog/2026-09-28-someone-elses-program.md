## Someone Else's Program

> Men are disturbed not by things, but by the views which they take of them.
>
> — Epictetus, Enchiridion 5

A stranger online with different genetics, different sleep, different years of training and a different job has a program that works beautifully for them.

Your coach can see your movement. The internet can't. That's the whole difference, and it is a large one.

**Today's challenge:** Bring one thing you read online to a coach and ask whether it fits you.
