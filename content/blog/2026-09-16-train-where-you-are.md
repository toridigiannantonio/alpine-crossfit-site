## Train Where You Are

> First say to yourself what you would be, and then do what you have to do.
>
> — Epictetus, Discourses 3.23.1

There is always a better split, a smarter cycle, a more optimal way to periodize. Chasing it is a way of feeling productive without training.

Three coached sessions a week, done for a year, beats every perfect plan abandoned in February. Consistency is not the boring part of the answer. It is the answer.

**Today's challenge:** Write down the number of sessions a week you can genuinely hold, and hold it.
