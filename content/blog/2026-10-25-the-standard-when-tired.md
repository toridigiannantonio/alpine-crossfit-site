## The Standard When Tired

> It is in times of security that the spirit should be preparing itself for difficult times.
>
> — Seneca, Letters to Lucilius 18

Everybody's squat is good on rep one. The question is what it looks like on rep forty, when the breathing is ragged and the mind is elsewhere.

That's the rep that gets people hurt, and the one that separates athletes who train for years from athletes who train for months.

**Today's challenge:** Call your own no-rep today rather than waiting for a coach to.
