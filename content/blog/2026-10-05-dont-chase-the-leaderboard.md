## Don't Chase the Leaderboard

> It is not the man who has too little, but the man who craves more, that is poor.
>
> — Seneca, Letters to Lucilius 2

Watching the fittest person in the room set the pace and deciding to match it is how a good workout becomes a bad week.

Their pace is the product of their training age. Match your own and you'll have theirs eventually.

**Today's challenge:** Set your pace in the first minute today and refuse to change it.
