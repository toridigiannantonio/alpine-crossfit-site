## Comparison Is a Tax

> If you wish to improve, be content to be thought foolish and stupid.
>
> — Epictetus, Enchiridion 13

There is always someone lifting more. In a good gym there are several, and they're right there, in your peripheral vision, making your working weight look modest.

That comparison takes attention away from your own set and gives you nothing back. Their numbers are the product of their years. Yours will be the product of yours.

**Today's challenge:** Cover the whiteboard column that isn't yours for one workout.
