## Volume Over Intensity

> Practice yourself, for heaven's sake, in little things.
>
> — Epictetus, Discourses 1.18

The instinct when progress stalls is to go harder. Usually the answer is duller: more sets at a manageable weight, done more often.

Intensity is exciting and produces fatigue quickly. Volume is boring and produces adaptation reliably.

**Today's challenge:** Add a set at your working weight rather than adding weight.
