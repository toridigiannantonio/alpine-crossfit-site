## Strong Enough for What

> To be everywhere is to be nowhere.
>
> — Seneca, Letters to Lucilius 2

Strong is not a destination. Strong enough to deadlift your bodyweight, to hike a fourteener, to keep up on a ski day — those are destinations.

Vague goals produce vague training. Specific ones tell you what to do on a Tuesday.

**Today's challenge:** Turn one vague goal into a number and a date.
