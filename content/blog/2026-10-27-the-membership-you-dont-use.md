## The Membership You Don't Use

> Every day, therefore, should be regulated as if it closed the series.
>
> — Seneca, Letters to Lucilius 12

Buying a membership is a statement of intent, and the intent decays quietly. Three weeks becomes three months without any single decision being made.

Nobody chooses to stop. They just don't choose to go, repeatedly, until it's a fact.

**Today's challenge:** If it's been a while, come back this week — not next week.
