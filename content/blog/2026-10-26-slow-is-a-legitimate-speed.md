## Slow Is a Legitimate Speed

> Nothing is more foolish than to be in haste.
>
> — Seneca, Letters to Lucilius

Some pieces are meant to be uncomfortable and fast. Many are meant to be steady, controlled and entirely sustainable.

Treating every session like a competition is a good way to be tired constantly and better at nothing in particular.

**Today's challenge:** Ask your coach what pace today's workout is actually meant to be done at.
