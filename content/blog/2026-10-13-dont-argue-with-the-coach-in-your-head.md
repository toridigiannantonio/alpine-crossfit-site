## Don't Argue With the Coach in Your Head

> It is not events that disturb people, it is their judgements concerning them.
>
> — Epictetus, Enchiridion 5

Mid-workout the mind produces a running commentary: this is too heavy, this is unfair, everyone else is ahead. It sounds like thinking and isn't.

You can hear it without agreeing with it. The commentary is weather passing through; the set is the thing that's real.

**Today's challenge:** Notice one unhelpful thought today and let it finish without acting on it.
