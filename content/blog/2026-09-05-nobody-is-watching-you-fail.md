## Nobody Is Watching You Fail

> We suffer more often in imagination than in reality.
>
> — Seneca, Letters to Lucilius 13

The fear that stops people signing up is the fear of being watched — of being the worst one there, visibly. It is almost entirely imaginary.

Everyone in a class is absorbed in their own breathing, their own rep count, their own suffering. The attention you're dreading does not exist. What does exist is a room of people who remember being new.

**Today's challenge:** Go to the class you've been avoiding because you thought you weren't ready for it.
