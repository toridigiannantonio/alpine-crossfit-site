## The Second Set

> No man is crushed by misfortune unless he has first been deceived by prosperity.
>
> — Seneca, On Providence 4

The first set feels fine. Everyone gets through the first set. Character shows up somewhere in the second, when the novelty is gone and the arithmetic of how much is left becomes real.

That's the rep where you decide what kind of athlete you are. Not the max attempt. The unremarkable middle.

**Today's challenge:** Notice the exact moment today when you first want to stop, and do three more reps past it.
