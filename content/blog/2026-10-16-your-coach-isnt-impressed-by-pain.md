## Your Coach Isn't Impressed by Pain

> It is not the man who has too little who is poor, but the man who craves more.
>
> — Seneca, Letters to Lucilius 2

There is a version of training that measures success by how wrecked you feel. It is popular, it produces stories, and it does not produce much fitness.

The goal is a stimulus large enough to force adaptation and small enough to recover from. That's it. Anything past that is just damage with good branding.

**Today's challenge:** End today's session when the work is done rather than when you're destroyed.
