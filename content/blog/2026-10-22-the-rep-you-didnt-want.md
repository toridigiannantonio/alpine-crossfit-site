## The Rep You Didn't Want

> Difficulties strengthen the mind, as labour does the body.
>
> — Seneca, Letters to Lucilius

There is always one more rep available than the one you wanted to stop at. Not ten more — the body's warnings are real — but one.

Finding that rep repeatedly is how the gap between what you feel capable of and what you're capable of slowly closes.

**Today's challenge:** Take one more rep than you planned on a single set today.
