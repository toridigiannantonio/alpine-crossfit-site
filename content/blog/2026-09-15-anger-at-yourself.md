## Anger at Yourself

> How much more grievous are the consequences of anger than the causes of it.
>
> — Marcus Aurelius, Meditations 11.18

A missed rep produces a small flare of self-directed anger, and that anger is more expensive than the miss. It tightens everything, ruins the next attempt, and follows you to the car.

The bar didn't move. That's information, not an insult. Reset, adjust one variable, go again.

**Today's challenge:** Miss something today and go straight to your next attempt without commentary.
