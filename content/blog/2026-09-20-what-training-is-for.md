## What Training Is For

> It is not that we have a short time to live, but that we waste a lot of it.
>
> — Seneca, On the Shortness of Life 1

Nobody's life gets better because of a number on a whiteboard. It gets better because they can carry the groceries, take the stairs, get down on the floor with a grandchild and get back up.

The training is instrumental. Keep the thing it's instrumental to in view, and the hard sessions stop feeling arbitrary.

**Today's challenge:** Name the specific thing outside the gym you want this to make possible.
