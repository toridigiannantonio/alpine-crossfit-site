## Show Up Unmotivated

> At dawn, when you have trouble getting out of bed, tell yourself: I have to go to work — as a human being.
>
> — Marcus Aurelius, Meditations 5.1

Marcus wrote a note to himself about not wanting to get up. The most disciplined man in the Roman Empire needed a written argument to leave his bed.

That should be reassuring. Not wanting to train is not a character flaw or a sign you should quit. It is the ordinary condition, and it has nothing to do with whether you go.

**Today's challenge:** Go on a day you don't feel like it, and let the session be mediocre.
