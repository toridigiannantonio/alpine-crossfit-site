## The Set You Don't Post

> Do not explain your philosophy. Embody it.
>
> — attributed to Epictetus

Very little of what makes someone strong is worth photographing. It is accessory work, warm-up sets, a boring hour on a Tuesday in February.

The parts that look good are the top of a very large pile of parts that don't. Enjoy the pile.

**Today's challenge:** Do today's least interesting movement with full attention.
