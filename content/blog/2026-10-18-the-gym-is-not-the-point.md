## The Gym Is Not the Point

> Waste no more time arguing what a good man should be. Be one.
>
> — Marcus Aurelius, Meditations 10.16

It's possible to make training the whole identity — to organise every meal, every evening, every holiday around it. That's not health, it's a different kind of imbalance.

The training is there so the rest of your life gets easier. If it starts consuming the rest of your life, something's inverted.

**Today's challenge:** Skip a session this month for something that matters more, without guilt.
