## Gratitude for a Working Body

> Think of what you have rather than of what you lack.
>
> — Marcus Aurelius, Meditations 7.27

The knees that ache going up the stairs are the knees that got you up the stairs. The tired shoulders held a barbell overhead this morning.

This is not a trick to feel better. It's an accurate description that resentment tends to leave out.

**Today's challenge:** Name one thing your body did today that you'd miss if it stopped.
