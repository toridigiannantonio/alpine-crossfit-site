## The Room Carries You

> We are made for cooperation, like feet, like hands, like eyelids.
>
> — Marcus Aurelius, Meditations 2.1

Doing the same workout alone in a garage is measurably harder. Not because the weight changes, but because nothing around you insists you keep going.

Turning up to a room where nine other people are suffering the same way is not a soft option. It is how humans have always done difficult things.

**Today's challenge:** Learn the name of someone in your class you haven't spoken to.
